HOW THE PROTOTYPE WORKS

Clear rules. No surprises.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Early-stage notice: Perkly is a prototype. These notes explain the current demo behaviour; they are not final legal terms and do not promise earnings, cash, vouchers or other benefits.

Using Perkly

Use a valid email address, keep your sign-in details private and provide accurate information. Do not impersonate others, submit harmful or unlawful content, automate activity submissions, or attempt to bypass the review system.

Activities and review

Activity cards describe possible tasks. A submission is only a request for review. Perkly may approve or reject it after checking the information provided. Repeated, misleading or incomplete submissions may be rejected. Some activities are demonstration examples and may not represent a paid partner campaign.

Points

Points are internal prototype units, not currency, deposits, wages or a promise of monetary value. Points are credited only after approval. A points balance does not represent money owed to you.

Rewards

Items labelled as previews or samples are not currently redeemable. No cash payment, voucher, product delivery, donation or other reward is guaranteed. Real reward requests require confirmed fulfilment partners, clear eligibility rules and an operational support process.

Partner enquiries

Submitting a partner enquiry expresses interest only. It does not create a survey campaign, purchase, service agreement or commitment to pay. Campaign terms and privacy requirements must be agreed separately before any real activity is offered.

Availability and changes

The prototype may change, pause or be unavailable while it is tested. Features may be removed or adjusted to protect account integrity and data accuracy. Do not use Perkly as a source of guaranteed income or for urgent financial needs.

Before public launch

The operator must publish complete legal terms, privacy and support contact details, eligibility rules, dispute handling, and any required consumer, tax, advertising or payment disclosures before offering real rewards or paid campaigns.
